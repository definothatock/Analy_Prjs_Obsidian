
> While Elden Ring shared similar rules, mechanics and aesthetic with Dark Souls and Sekiro, Elden Ring was much more successful somehow, why?

# Quick Review / Abstract

- **Unit of Analysis:** _Elden Ring_ as a full project, with backward comparison to _Dark Souls_ and _Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice_.
- **Analytical Focus:** Why its established Souls formula crossed from enthusiast prestige into mainstream/global commercial visibility.
- **Core Finding:** _Elden Ring_ did not become global simply by making Souls “easier.” It preserved the high-stakes combat identity, but changed the **structure around failure**: a player blocked by one challenge could explore, level, change build, summon help, find upgrades, or return later. This substantially widened the viable decision space without removing the mastery ceiling.
- **Major Strength:** It combined a distinctive, demanding combat system with an open-world adventure framework that was legible and desirable to a much broader market.
- **Major Risk:** Its breadth sometimes weakens the concentrated pacing and authored encounter density that made earlier FromSoftware games especially cohesive.

# 0. Scope and Framing

## 0.1 Unit of Analysis

**Full Project:** _Elden Ring_ (2022), primarily compared with the _Dark Souls_ series and _Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice_.

## 0.2 Analytical Priority

- Structural understanding
- Experiential understanding
- Comparative evaluation
- Audience-fit explanation

## 0.3 Evidence Basis

This analysis distinguishes:

- **Observed structure:** open exploration, mounted traversal, build variety, optional encounters, co-op, spirit summons, frequent checkpoints, and high-consequence combat.
- **Developer-stated intention:** Miyazaki described the project in terms of adventure, exploration of the unknown, freedom, and balancing difficulty around that freedom. ([gamespot.com](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/hidetaka-miyazaki-on-bringing-elden-ring-to-a-close-and-the-future-of-from-software/1100-6524337/?utm_source=openai))
- **Market evidence:** _Elden Ring_ exceeded 12 million units worldwide within roughly three weeks of release; it later exceeded 30 million units. By contrast, _Sekiro_ reached 10 million lifetime units by September 2023. ([en.bandainamcoent.eu](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/elden-ring-reaches-12-million-units-sold-worldwide?utm_source=openai))
- **Interpretation:** The game’s global reach emerged from the interaction of design, accumulated studio reputation, recognisable fantasy presentation, distribution, and unusually strong social circulation.

First, a small correction to the premise: **Souls was no longer commercially tiny before _Elden Ring_**. In June 2019, FromSoftware stated that the _Dark Souls_ series had cumulatively shipped more than 25 million copies. _Sekiro_ also eventually passed 10 million. But the series was still comparatively **subcultural**: highly respected, highly discussed among dedicated players, but not yet a broadly assumed “everyone should try this” blockbuster. ([fromsoftware.jp](https://www.fromsoftware.jp/ww/pressrelease/20190610_eldenring_debut.html?utm_source=openai))

So the question is not “why did an unknown niche studio suddenly become popular?” It is:

> **Why did a respected, already successful specialist lineage suddenly become a mass-market cultural event?**

# 1. Core Components

## 1.1 Core Mechanics

- **Actor Relation Structure:** `#Single`, `#PvE`, with meaningful `#CoOp` and `#PvP` options.
- **Core Mechanics:** third-person melee/ranged combat; stamina and positioning management; dodge/block/parry timing; character-build construction; mounted traversal; dungeon exploration; optional boss encounters; asynchronous/synchronous multiplayer.
- **Progression/Loop Label:** `#OpenWorld`.

“Action RPG” and “Soulslike” are useful community labels, but they obscure the important structural point: _Elden Ring_ is a combat-challenge project embedded within a broad exploratory framework.

## 1.2 Player Verbs

The player repeatedly:

- Explore
- Ride
- Fight
- Dodge
- Block / parry
- Cast
- Summon
- Loot
- Equip
- Upgrade
- Level
- Compare builds
- Retreat
- Mark locations
- Return later

The unusually important verbs are **retreat**, **redirect**, and **return later**. Those are central to why the game appealed beyond the previous audience.

## 1.3 Core Resources

- Health and healing charges
- Runes: both currency and level-up resource
- Stamina / FP
- Weapons, armor, spells, Ashes of War
- Upgrade materials
- Map knowledge and discovered locations
- Time and player attention

## 1.4 Game Verbs

The game repeatedly:

- Ambushes
- Pursues
- Punishes poor spacing or timing
- Gates routes with powerful enemies
- Reveals optional paths and bosses
- Escalates enemy damage, durability, and moveset complexity
- Rewards exploration with tools that alter later combat possibilities

# 2. Gameplay Framework

## 2.1 Play Objective / Success State

The formal objective is to progress through the Lands Between, defeat central bosses, and reach an ending.

But the practical player objective is often more flexible:

> **Become capable enough—through skill, knowledge, tools, or character growth—to overcome the next chosen obstacle.**

That wording matters. In a more linear game, the next obstacle may be the only meaningful route forward. In _Elden Ring_, “become capable” has multiple routes.

## 2.2 Failure Pressure

- Death and rune loss
- Boss defeat
- Resource depletion
- Getting lost or entering dangerous regions underprepared
- Repeated failure against high-skill encounters
- Opportunity cost: choosing one region, build, upgrade path, or quest branch over another

The game remains severe. It does not eliminate failure pressure. It **redistributes** how often failure becomes a total stop.

## 2.3 Core Loop

1. The player identifies an unknown location, route, enemy, dungeon, or boss.
2. They commit to exploration or combat.
3. The game tests execution, build adequacy, preparation, and information.
4. The player receives defeat, discovery, loot, runes, shortcuts, map knowledge, or a new route.
5. They either retry, alter strategy, seek assistance, explore elsewhere, or return stronger.
6. The world continues to offer additional opportunities.

This is the crucial change:

- Earlier Souls games often used a loop closer to:  
    **advance → fail at obstacle → learn/rebuild → retry obstacle.**
- _Elden Ring_ often uses:  
    **discover → fail or succeed → redirect → gain capability/information → return or proceed elsewhere.**

That change made the game less psychologically exclusionary without making its combat uniformly easy.

## 2.4 Progression Structure

_Elden Ring_ combines several progression forms:

- Skill mastery
- Character levels
- Weapon upgrades
- Spell/weapon/build acquisition
- Map knowledge
- Optional boss rewards
- Social knowledge: guides, messages, community builds, boss strategies

This layered progression is broader than _Sekiro_, whose protagonist, combat grammar, and character growth are much more fixed. _Sekiro_ offers depth, but less strategic rerouting through character construction.

# 3. Central System Analysis

## 3.1 Central Distinctive Systems

The three systems most relevant to mainstream expansion are:

1. **Open-world exploration and redirection**
2. **Combat/build plurality**
3. **Socially distributed discovery and assistance**

---

## 3.2 System A — Open-World Exploration and Redirection

### A. Function

This system converts a blocked combat challenge into a navigational and strategic choice.

Instead of asking only, “Can you beat this boss now?”, it often lets the player ask:

- Should I fight it now?
- Can I find another region?
- Can I gain runes or upgrade materials?
- Can I change weapons or spells?
- Can I find another summon, dungeon, NPC, or route?
- Is this enemy intended for later?

### B. Structure

The system consists of:

- Large connected regions
- Optional caves, catacombs, ruins, evergaols, legacy dungeons, and field bosses
- Mounted movement
- Map fragments and points of interest
- Fast travel and Sites of Grace
- Multiple paths into later content
- Rewards distributed throughout exploration

Bandai Namco explicitly marketed the Lands Between as FromSoftware’s largest world to date and highlighted co-op, broad exploration, and unexpected discoveries. ([bandainamcoent.com](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/elden-ring-now-available-adventure-arrives-for-the-brave?utm_source=openai))

### C. Functional Role

The open world is not merely “more content.” Its structural role is to create **failure elasticity**.

A linear difficult game has a high risk of a hard stop:

> “I cannot do the required thing, therefore I cannot continue.”

_Elden Ring_ often changes that to:

> “I cannot do this now, but I can still play meaningfully.”

That is one of the most important reasons it could retain FromSoftware’s demanding identity while becoming more approachable.

### D. Interactions

Exploration outputs become combat inputs:

- A dungeon gives upgrade materials.
- An optional enemy gives runes.
- A side area gives a weapon or summon.
- A new region gives a different type of encounter.
- A map discovery gives future strategic options.

Thus, combat failure can create exploration behavior, and exploration can create future combat capability.

### E. Legibility / Information Structure

The world is partly legible through visible landmarks, map fragments, environmental silhouettes, enemy threat, and player-made messages. It is still intentionally opaque in quests and hidden mechanics.

This partial legibility is useful for broad audiences because the player usually has a visible direction even when they do not understand the full narrative or optimal route.

### F. Friction / Breakdown

The same openness can create:

- Uneven difficulty order
- Accidental overleveling or underleveling
- Reused minor bosses
- Diffuse pacing
- More dependence on external guides for obscure quests or builds

So openness increased accessibility of _continuing to play_, but not necessarily clarity of all systems.

---

## 3.3 System B — Combat Challenge with Build Plurality

### A. Function

Combat maintains the distinctive FromSoftware promise: high tension, readable but dangerous enemy patterns, strong punishment for poor decisions, and unusually memorable victories.

But _Elden Ring_ gives the player more methods for approaching those encounters than _Sekiro_ does.

### B. Structure

Relevant components include:

- Melee weapon classes
- Shields and parries
- Sorcery and incantations
- Status effects
- Ashes of War
- Spirit Ashes
- Consumables
- Co-op summons
- Attribute allocation and equipment load

The player is not only improving reaction timing. They can change the combat problem itself.

### C. Functional Role

This system widens the **decision space around difficulty**.

A boss can remain mechanically demanding while becoming more approachable through:

- A different damage type
- More vigor or upgraded weapon level
- Range rather than melee
- Status effects
- A tanking or distracting spirit summon
- Another human player
- Better positioning or a different weapon skill

That is different from simply lowering enemy health or damage. It creates more legitimate solution paths.

### D. Interactions

This system depends heavily on the exploration system:

- Exploration supplies tools.
- Tools support builds.
- Builds change combat behavior.
- Combat victory opens further exploration and progression.

This is a reinforcing loop. The player who enjoys exploration gains more ways to engage with combat; the player who enjoys combat has more reasons to explore.

### E. Legibility / Information Structure

At a basic level, the game communicates combat well enough: attacks have animations, spacing matters, damage is visible, and death is immediate feedback.

At a higher level, build effectiveness is much less legible. Weapon scaling, status thresholds, hidden quest conditions, and upgrade efficiency are often learned through experimentation or community knowledge.

### F. Friction / Breakdown

Build breadth has costs:

- Balance varies greatly between tools.
- Some strong strategies can reduce the need to engage deeply with an encounter.
- New players may not know which tools are available to them.
- A player can mistake hidden options for personal failure.

Still, commercially, this breadth is advantageous: it permits players with different tastes and skill profiles to find a viable relationship with the same game.

---

## 3.4 System C — Social Discovery, Co-op, and Spectatorship

### A. Function

This system turns individual difficulty and mystery into shareable material.

The player can:

- Summon others
- Leave or read messages
- Watch builds, lore videos, challenge runs, boss guides, and reaction clips
- Share discoveries and failures
- Participate in memes around bosses, NPCs, and unusual player behavior

### B. Structure

The project combines official co-op/PvP support with an internet-friendly game structure:

- Distinct bosses
- Strong visual reveals
- Hidden discoveries
- Build experimentation
- Unusual deaths
- Easy-to-explain “I found this insane thing” moments
- A shared vocabulary around bosses, weapons, and regions

The official release material emphasized both cooperative and competitive multiplayer. ([bandainamcoent.com](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/elden-ring-now-available-adventure-arrives-for-the-brave?utm_source=openai))

### C. Functional Role

The social layer lowers entry friction in a different way from an in-game tutorial.

A prospective player does not need to understand every rule before buying. They can first understand the game through:

- Friends
- Streamers
- Short clips
- Wiki pages
- Build recommendations
- Shared boss stories

In this sense, the wider community became a supporting information system for an intentionally incomplete in-game information structure.

### D. Friction / Breakdown

This can also weaken self-discovery:

- Players may copy dominant builds.
- Guides can flatten mystery and experimentation.
- Social pressure can turn challenge into performance.
- Online discourse can exaggerate “correct” ways to play.

But it also made a previously specialist style much easier to enter.

# 4. Why It Became Global: Deductive Explanation from Different Perspectives

## 4.1 Design Perspective — It Changed the Meaning of Being Stuck

This is the strongest explanation.

Earlier Souls titles already offered builds, summons, side routes, and exploration. The difference is **degree and framework position**.

In a more compact or linear Souls structure, a difficult mandatory boss can dominate the player’s session. The player may farm, summon, respec only in some entries, or seek help—but the immediate experience is still “beat this wall.”

In _Elden Ring_, the world itself is an alternative response to failure.

Therefore:

1. The game retains difficult enemies and bosses.
2. But failure against one enemy does not necessarily suspend all progress.
3. The player can transform frustration into another pleasurable activity: exploration, loot acquisition, experimentation, or social play.
4. More players remain engaged long enough to develop skill, attachment, and investment.
5. A larger retained audience produces more word-of-mouth and cultural visibility.

**Conclusion:** _Elden Ring_ did not simply lower difficulty; it reduced the frequency with which difficulty becomes an absolute exit point.

Miyazaki’s own description supports this reading: he framed the project around freedom and adventure, and stated that balancing difficulty with that freedom was a core development problem. ([gamespot.com](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/hidetaka-miyazaki-on-bringing-elden-ring-to-a-close-and-the-future-of-from-software/1100-6524337/?utm_source=openai))

---

## 4.2 Audience Perspective — It Offered Multiple Player Fantasies at Once

_Sekiro_ is exceptionally focused. Its main fantasy is roughly:

> Become a highly disciplined swordsman who can read and answer aggressive enemy pressure.

That focus is artistically strong, but it narrows audience fit. A player who dislikes parry-centered timing, fixed protagonist identity, or constrained build expression has fewer alternate relationships with the game.

_Elden Ring_ offers several overlapping fantasies:

- Be a wandering explorer
- Be a knight with a colossal sword
- Be a mage
- Be a stealthy assassin
- Be a collector
- Be a co-op helper
- Be a lore investigator
- Be a min-max builder
- Be a player who eventually defeats a feared boss through persistence

This does not mean every player will enjoy every system. It means the game has more **entry points**.

A player can initially arrive for open-world exploration, fantasy scenery, George R. R. Martin’s involvement, or co-op—and only later become invested in the Souls combat loop.

---

## 4.3 Market Perspective — The Studio Had Already Earned Trust, Then Presented a Mass-Market Package

_Elden Ring_ had a much stronger starting position than the original _Demon’s Souls_ or _Dark Souls_.

Before release:

- FromSoftware had accumulated a large and committed audience through the _Dark Souls_ lineage.
- The _Dark Souls_ series had surpassed 25 million cumulative shipments by June 2019.
- _Sekiro_ had already demonstrated that FromSoftware could gain major awards attention and reach millions outside the Souls brand. ([fromsoftware.jp](https://www.fromsoftware.jp/ww/pressrelease/20190610_eldenring_debut.html?utm_source=openai))

Then _Elden Ring_ added several mass-market signals:

- A new, easily marketable fantasy universe rather than “Dark Souls IV”
- The “largest FromSoftware game” framing
- A simultaneous cross-generation launch across PlayStation, Xbox, and PC
- Bandai Namco’s global publishing and marketing infrastructure
- George R. R. Martin’s name, which made the game intelligible to audiences who did not already care about Souls combat. ([bandainamcoent.com](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/an-adventure-awaits-the-brave-and-gallant-in-elden-ring?utm_source=openai))

The Martin collaboration should not be overstated as the sole cause. Many buyers likely did not purchase primarily because of his writing. But it acted as a **legitimacy and curiosity bridge**:

> “This is not merely a hardcore action game; this is a large fantasy event.”

That is valuable before anyone has played it.

---

## 4.4 Presentation Perspective — It Was Easier to Desire at a Glance

Older Souls games are atmospheric, but often visually communicate enclosure, decay, oppression, and repetition of death.

_Elden Ring_ still has those qualities, but its public-facing image is broader:

- Vast landscapes
- Huge castles
- Golden trees
- Dragons
- Mounted combat
- Giant enemies
- Striking character designs
- Distinct regions with strong visual contrast

This matters because mass audiences often decide whether to investigate a game before they understand its mechanics.

“Hard dark fantasy dungeon crawler” is appealing to a narrower group than:

> “A gigantic fantasy world where you can ride across fields toward castles, dragons, underground cities, and strange gods.”

The second fantasy is immediately legible to players of open-world RPGs even if they have never touched _Dark Souls_.

---

## 4.5 Cultural Perspective — It Generated an Unusually Strong Conversation Machine

Global popularity is partly a feedback loop.

1. The game creates surprising encounters and difficult bosses.
2. Those moments are easy to clip, stream, explain, meme, or argue about.
3. Visibility creates curiosity among non-players.
4. New players create more stories, builds, guides, and reactions.
5. The game becomes culturally difficult to ignore.

Its structure was especially good for this because every player’s route and build could differ while the broad landmarks remained shared. One player finds an underground city; another discovers a bizarre weapon; another spends days on a boss; another helps strangers defeat that boss.

That creates both **individual stories** and **collective recognition**.

The game’s early sales show how quickly this loop became self-reinforcing: it surpassed 12 million worldwide units by March 16, 2022, less than three weeks after its February 25 launch. ([en.bandainamcoent.eu](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/elden-ring-reaches-12-million-units-sold-worldwide?utm_source=openai))

---

## 4.6 Timing Perspective — It Met Existing Demand Rather Than Creating It Alone

This is an inference, not a directly measurable single cause.

By 2022, several audience expectations had converged:

- Open-world exploration was a dominant and familiar market form.
- Cross-platform online discourse around games was unusually central.
- Streaming and short-form clip culture strongly rewarded spectacular encounters and player reactions.
- Players were receptive to large “event” releases with long tails of guides, builds, lore, and repeat discussion.

_Elden Ring_ entered that environment with a form that was simultaneously familiar—an open-world fantasy action RPG—and distinctive—its hostile world, combat stakes, opaque lore, and lack of conventional checklist design.

Its achievement was not inventing the open world. It was using the open world to make the FromSoftware challenge structure more expandable.

# 5. Interpretation and Evaluation

## 5.1 Project Identity

_Elden Ring_ appears to pursue:

> **A dangerous, mysterious fantasy expedition in which overcoming adversity can come from mechanical mastery, preparation, exploration, character construction, cooperation, or persistence.**

That is meaningfully broader than the identity of _Sekiro_, which is more concentrated around direct martial mastery, and broader than the tighter route structures associated with much of _Dark Souls_.

## 5.2 Target Evaluation

### Primary audience

- Existing FromSoftware/Souls players
- Open-world RPG players
- Dark-fantasy fans
- Players seeking high challenge and achievement
- Build-crafting and theorycrafting players
- Co-op and online-community players

### Why it served them effectively

For veterans, it offered scale, discovery, build variety, and more elaborate boss spectacle without abandoning the familiar combat language.

For newcomers, it offered more ways to participate without requiring immediate mastery of one narrow combat solution.

For open-world players, it provided a world that was less checklist-driven and more discovery-driven than many genre competitors.

This is why the project could expand outward without fully abandoning its original audience.

## 5.3 Design Alignment

The project is mostly well aligned.

- **Combat pressure** supports danger and achievement.
- **Exploration rewards** support curiosity and experimentation.
- **Build progression** supports multiple solution paths.
- **Co-op and summons** support flexible difficulty management.
- **Opaque lore and hidden areas** support mystery and communal interpretation.

The major alignment achievement is that accessibility comes largely through **options**, not through a separate low-pressure mode that would fundamentally alter the project’s tone.

## 5.4 Communication Effectiveness

The game communicates immediate combat consequences effectively but communicates larger systems unevenly.

Effective communication:

- Enemy aggression
- Damage
- Stagger
- Death
- Visible landmarks
- Map expansion
- Checkpoints
- Item discovery

Weak communication:

- Quest progression
- NPC location changes
- Some build/stat interactions
- Hidden mechanics
- Relative regional difficulty
- The full implications of available tools

This is partly intentional: obscurity supports mystery and social discovery. But it can become noise when players do not realise that the game offers alternatives to their current obstacle.

## 5.5 Distinctive Strengths

1. **Difficulty with alternatives:** hard encounters remain meaningful, but players have many ways to prepare or redirect.
2. **Exploration as progression:** exploration is not merely scenic; it materially changes future decision space.
3. **Strong player stories:** the game reliably generates memorable anecdotes.
4. **Broad fantasy readability:** its world is immediately appealing even to non-Souls players.
5. **Retention through curiosity:** players who are temporarily blocked still have reasons to continue.

## 5.6 Design Risks / Weaknesses

1. **Pacing diffusion:** the open world can weaken the focused escalation of tightly designed linear progression.
2. **Content repetition:** breadth can require reuse of encounter structures.
3. **Balance variance:** some builds, summons, or tools can trivialize encounters relative to others.
4. **External-information dependence:** the game’s community can compensate for incomplete in-game explanation, but that means solitary players may miss viable paths.
5. **Identity tension:** some veteran players may prefer the density, discipline, and authored encounter sequencing of _Sekiro_ or the best legacy dungeons.

# 6. Evaluative Comparison

## 6.1 Backward Comparison: _Elden Ring_ vs. _Dark Souls_

### What stayed the same

- Deliberate stamina-based combat
- High consequence for mistakes
- Environmental mystery
- Boss-centered climaxes
- Build-based character expression
- Co-op/PvP foundations
- Achievement through learning

### What changed

- A much larger exploratory framework
- Mounted traversal
- Greater ability to route around failure
- More build tools and combat approaches
- More optional content
- More visible spectacle and broader fantasy appeal

### Principle shift

The older structure often asks:

> “Can you pass this designed sequence?”

_Elden Ring_ more often asks:

> “How will you prepare yourself, choose a route, and eventually solve this world?”

That is a substantial audience expansion.

---

## 6.2 Orthogonal Comparison: Why Open-World RPG Players Could Join

Players who normally prefer games such as broad exploration RPGs may not be attracted to Souls-style attrition and boss pressure.

But _Elden Ring_ overlaps with them through:

- Large-scale landscape discovery
- Character builds
- World lore
- Loot
- Optional content
- Mount traversal
- Self-directed objectives
- A sense of journey rather than only combat trials

The game therefore extended into another audience without becoming structurally identical to a conventional open-world RPG.

---

# 7. Outcome Summary

**Why did _Elden Ring_ become global?**

Not because _Dark Souls_ and _Sekiro_ were failures or completely obscure—they had already built a large, respected foundation. And not because _Elden Ring_ simply made Souls easy.

It became global because it combined:

1. **An accumulated FromSoftware reputation**
2. **A recognisable large-scale fantasy package**, amplified by George R. R. Martin and global Bandai Namco marketing
3. **Cross-platform reach**
4. **An open-world framework that transformed hard stops into alternate choices**
5. **Build, summon, co-op, and exploration options that widened the decision space**
6. **A highly shareable structure of bosses, secrets, discoveries, failures, memes, and guides**
7. **A core combat identity strong enough to remain distinctive amid familiar open-world conventions**

The shortest version is:

> _Elden Ring_ made the Souls experience less **mandatory-linear** without making it less **intense**.  
> It let more kinds of players enter, stay, and eventually claim the same feeling of overcoming something difficult.
